# The Bedbug Plan: Song Generation Prompt

This document explains "Operation Clean Slate," a tactical bedbug remediation plan coordinated remotely by a son (Ben) for his mother (Mom). Use this context to generate a song (e.g., Americana, Folk-Rock, or Synth-Pop vibe).

## 1. The Narrative Setup
*   **The Conflict:** Mom thinks she has bedbugs. She's anxious and ready to spend $1,000 on an exterminator.
*   **The Hero:** Ben, coordinating from another state, suspicious it might just be dry skin or a scam, but ready with a rigorous tactical plan.
*   **The Setting:** A small apartment in Florida. A bedroom turned into a "Quarantine Zone" and a living room turned into a "Clean Zone."
*   **The Vibe:** Methodical, protective, slightly obsessive, but deeply caring. It's a "son-protecting-mom" story told through laundry cycles and plastic bags.

## 2. Key Tactical Concepts (The "Lore")
*   **Operation Clean Slate:** The name of the mission.
*   **The Golden Rule:** "Heat kills, plastic contains, time finishes."
*   **The Two-Zone Strategy:**
    *   **The Bedroom (Quarantine):** Sealed off. Towel under the door. A "holding cell."
    *   **The Living Room (Clean):** Where Mom lives now. A new ZenDen mattress on a Cozy City frame, floating away from the walls.
*   **The Interceptors:** 6 black cups under the bed legs. The morning ritual is checking these for "captures."
*   **The Laundry Workflow (The Workhorse):**
    *   "Dry first, then wash, then dry again."
    *   High heat for 45 minutes is the "kill step."
    *   Double-bagging everything in 55-gallon contractor bags.
*   **Exile:** Instead of chemicals, just close the bedroom door for 18 months. Let biology do the work. Starve them out.

## 3. Recurring Imagery & Phrases
*   **"Apple-seed sized"** (the bugs).
*   **"Breakfast, lunch, and dinner"** (the linear bite pattern).
*   **"6 black cups"** (the interceptors).
*   **"5 taps on the header"** (the secret way to Ben’s help).
*   **"One load a day."**
*   **"Don't trash the sleigh bed yet."**

## 4. The "5 Rules" (Potential Chorus/Bridge)
1.  **Bedroom is Dirty, Living Room is Clean.**
2.  **Entering the bedroom?** Special clothes and booties first.
3.  **Coming out?** Strip, bag, shower, fresh Ziploc clothes.
4.  **Dryer first (High Heat, 45 min).**
5.  **Every morning:** Check the cups for captures.

## 5. Emotional Core
The song shouldn't just be about bugs. It’s about the distance between two people, the "9 AM check-in call," and the way we use checklists and protocols to show love when we can't be there to move the mattress ourselves.

---
**Suggested Song Structure:**
*   **Verse 1:** The panic, the $1,000 quote, the suspicion of a scam.
*   **Chorus:** The mantra ("Heat kills, plastic contains, time finishes") and the 9 AM call.
*   **Verse 2:** The setup. Moving to the living room. The ZenDen and the 6 black cups.
*   **Bridge:** The laundry ritual. Double-bags and 45 minutes on high.
*   **Chorus:** (Repeat)
*   **Outro:** The 18-month wait, the towel under the door.
